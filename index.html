How to Use ShareAble
Share files instantly — no accounts, no cloud.
1
Pick Your Avatar
Enter your name and choose a cute avatar so your friend can recognise you on their screen.
2
Choose a Role
One person picks Send, the other picks Receive. Both need to be on this page at the same time.
3
🌐 Public or 🔒 Private Radar
Public (default): anyone using ShareAble right now can see you and connect. Private Room: enter a short code and share it with your friend however you like — text, call, in person. Only someone with that exact code can see or connect to you.
4
Connect
Public: click your friend's blip on the radar, or share your Room ID directly and have them type it in and hit Connect. Private: just wait — once your codes match, a confirm popup pops up automatically, one tap and you're in.
5
Send Files — Either Side!
Once connected, either person can send. Click "I want to Send" any time — even after connecting — to drag in files and hit Send. Progress stays in sync on both ends, and switching who's sending never disconnects you.
6
File Saves Automatically
The receiver's file downloads straight to their device when transfer is complete. No sign-up. No storage limits. 🎉
